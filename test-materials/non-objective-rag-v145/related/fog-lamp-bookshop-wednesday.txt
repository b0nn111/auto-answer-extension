雾灯书屋的周三记录

This long archive note is intentionally fictional. It says Alice cleaned the 蓝色抽屉 before closing. She wrote that the snack in the drawer was not 薄荷糖, not 肉桂饼干, and not 柠檬蛋糕. The item reserved for the early visitor was 苹果干. This is the only snack named as the real drawer item.

Bao handled the 旧城地图夹. To stop people mixing it with drawing copies, Bao added two labels: 灰色棉线 on the upper-left corner and 绿色回形针 on the back cover. The same paragraph warns that 红色蜡封 and 小月亮贴纸 belong to other collections and are distractors.

A later paragraph explains the 银色书签. Alice did not give it to the 馆长 because Mina needed it to check page numbers in 北窗笔记. If it went to the curator, it would be locked in the lost-property office. Therefore Alice handed the bookmark to Mina first.
