Class Notes - Quick Facts

Geography:
The capital of France is Paris. London is the capital of the United Kingdom, not France.

Astronomy:
Mars is called the Red Planet. The nickname comes from the reddish color of its surface.

Chemistry:
Water has the formula H2O. CO2 means carbon dioxide. NaCl means sodium chloride.
